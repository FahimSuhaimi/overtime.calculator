Enter your basic salary and overtime hours.

Normal day ? Monday to Friday, plus your own Saturday (week 1 and 3). Work hours are 8.30am to 5.30pm, so only the hours after 5.30pm count as overtime, at 1.5×.

Off day ? A day you should be off, like covering the other group's Saturday. The whole shift (8.30am to 5.30pm) counts as overtime, starting at 1.5×. Ask finance when the 2× and 3× rates apply.

Public holiday ? Working on a public holiday. Your finance sheet uses 2× and 3× for these hours. Ask finance which hours use which rate.
